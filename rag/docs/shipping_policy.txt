# RetailGPT Corporate Shipping Policy

## 1. Shipping Modes and Speed Guidelines
Our store offers four primary delivery options for customers. Shipping transit times are computed from the date the package is handed over to our shipping carriers:
* **Standard Class:** This is our default shipping method. Standard Class shipments are delivered in 4 to 6 business days.
* **Second Class:** An expedited ground shipping option. Second Class shipments are delivered in 2 to 3 business days.
* **First Class:** Our premium air delivery option. First Class shipments are delivered in exactly 1 business day (Next Day Delivery).
* **Same Day:** Our fastest express option. Same Day shipments are dispatched immediately and delivered on the same business day, provided the order is placed before 12:00 PM local time.

## 2. Shipping Carrier Partners
To ensure reliable global logistics, RetailGPT partners with the following standard shipping carriers:
* **Federal Express (FedEx):** Utilized primarily for Second Class and First Class shipments within North America.
* **United Parcel Service (UPS):** Utilized for Standard Class ground transport.
* **USPS (United States Postal Service):** Utilized for domestic Standard Class PO Box delivery.
* **DHL Express:** Dedicated carrier partner for all international markets and overseas shipments.

## 3. Shipping Rates and Cost Calculations
* Standard Class: Free for orders over $50, otherwise a flat fee of $4.95 is charged.
* Second Class: Computed as 5% of the total order value, with a minimum charge of $9.95.
* First Class: Computed as 12% of the total order value, with a minimum charge of $19.95.
* Same Day Delivery: Fixed flat rate of $29.99 for all orders, regardless of size or weight.
* Surcharges: An additional $15.00 heavy-item surcharge applies to Furniture items (specifically Tables, Chairs, and Bookcases) due to weight limits.
